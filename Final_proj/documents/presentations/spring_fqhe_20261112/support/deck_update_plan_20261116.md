## Why this update needs more than deleting frame 5

The old wall physics appears in frame 1, the frame-2 image, frame 5, frame 6, frames 16/18/19/21, README, build assertions and numerical manifests. Keeping any of its result numbers while dropping its physical context would silently change the benchmark being claimed. Preserve archived files but do not include them in the revised main PDF.

The new NN has a shared six-layer width-64 tanh backbone with additive readouts, then 42 independent private two-layer width-16 tanh heads. In `learned_sector_nonlinear_model.py`, each head multiplies the base amplitude by `1 + output_real + i output_imag`; a softmax gives learned real mixing weights and the physical state is their coherent weighted sum. The 294,464 real parameters therefore cannot be explained by the old 22,850-parameter diagram alone. Head labels are training targets, not exact angular projectors.

`l0_positive_penalty_runtime.py` implements the physical coherent-state positive K estimate using Lplus output samples and every incoming parent. Its actual loss is physical energy + the retained angular-head auxiliary loss + kappa times coherent K. A slide presenting the ideal H+lambda C evolution should identify this actual auxiliary term rather than claim the implementation optimizes only E+lambda K.
